FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the factory and product. We look for factories that accept trial quantities and tell you the MOQ with each quotation.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. We arrange samples before bulk production. Sample and courier costs are quoted upfront.

How does payment work?

Terms are agreed per order and stated clearly in the quotation, typically a deposit before production and the balance before shipment.

Which shipping terms do you support?

EXW, FOB and CIF are common. Tell us your destination and we'll quote accordingly.
